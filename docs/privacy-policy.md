# Privacy Policy for Quran Daily

**Last updated:** 21st Aug 2026

Quran Daily ("the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## Data collection

Quran Daily does not require account creation, sign-in, or any form of registration. Most app data stays entirely on your device (see below). The app does display advertisements, which involves some data collection by our advertising partner (see "Advertising" section below).

## Data stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device only, and is never transmitted to us:

- Which days you have marked an ayah as read (used to display your weekly reading tracker)
- Ayaat you have saved/bookmarked for later viewing
- Your preferred daily reminder notification time
- Your preferred display language (English or Bengali)

This data stays on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data is permanently deleted.

## Advertising

This app displays ads served by Google AdMob to support free access to the app. AdMob may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. This data is processed by Google, not by us directly.

You can review Google's data practices at:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Depending on your device settings, you may be able to limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings (e.g. "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

## Notifications

The app schedules local daily reminder notifications directly on your device to show you an ayah at your chosen time. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device and do not involve any external server or push notification service.

## Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. See the "Advertising" section above for details on what data this involves. The app does not integrate any other third-party analytics or tracking services.

## Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions, used only for the purposes described:

- **Notifications** — to display your daily ayah reminder
- **Exact alarm scheduling** — to ensure your daily reminder fires at your chosen time
- **Battery optimization exemption (optional)** — to improve reliability of daily reminders on devices that aggressively restrict background apps; you may decline this without losing app functionality

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described in the "Advertising" section above (which applies broadly via the ad network, not specifically to children).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
